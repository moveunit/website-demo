Ledger — consumer banking app

Cutting sign-up down to one honest question at a time

Lead product designer · 5-month engagement

Ledger's onboarding asked for nine fields before a new user saw any value. I split the flow into a single-question-per-screen sequence, moved identity verification to the point it actually became necessary, and replaced a wall of legal copy with inline explanations written at a sixth-grade reading level. Every change shipped as an A/B test against the existing flow.

interaction design onboarding A/B testing
+34%sign-up completion
−41smedian time to first deposit

Transit — self-service kiosk system

Redesigning a kiosk that had to work for everyone, first try

Product designer, hardware + software · 8-month engagement

A regional transit agency's ticket kiosks had a 22% abandonment rate and no accessible mode. I redesigned the touchscreen interface around a single default path — buy the most common fare in three taps — with a persistent high-contrast and screen-reader mode built in from the start rather than bolted on, and tested it with riders at three stations before rollout.

accessibility hardware UI field testing
41savg. transaction, down from 94s
−58%support calls per kiosk

Atlas — internal design system

Giving six product teams one shared vocabulary

Design systems lead · ongoing since 2024

Before Atlas, every team at the company had its own version of a dropdown. I built the token structure, component library, and contribution process from scratch, working with one engineer embedded full-time, and ran office hours every week so teams could bring edge cases instead of quietly forking components.
